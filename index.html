We map how institutions are connected, through shared directors, ownership, lobbying and funding, using only official public records, and we measure what we find against published standards. Balance, transparency and integrity, without bias or fear-mongering.

Identity · Transparency · Integrity

The three cores

Non-negotiable in every project, every record and every report.

1

Identity

Every organization and person is tied to an official registry ID. Nothing is joined by name alone: a match needs two independent facts, and uncertain matches wait for a human.

2

Transparency

Every connection links to the primary public record it comes from. Secondary sources are leads, never evidence. No source link, no finding.

3

Integrity

Nothing is changed silently. Corrections are new, dated records that point to the old ones; every record is fingerprinted in a tamper-evident log.

How we publish

Signals for review, not findings of wrongdoing.

  1. Attributed to the record. Findings read "filings report X as director of Y", each with its link.
  2. Checked twice. Every report is drafted, then independently re-checked: every source re-opened, every record confirmed unchanged, the wording screened.
  3. Right of reply. Every organization a finding concerns is invited to respond first, and responses are published in full.
  4. Open corrections. Found an error? Post it here or write to us. Corrections are dated, and earlier versions stay available.
