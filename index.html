Systems Assurance Forensic Investigation & Design

Independent research and design for verifiable trust: systems where people can check what is true instead of being asked to believe it, across AI safety, scientific claims, biological research, governance architecture and institutional accountability.

Identity · Transparency · Integrity

The three cores

Non-negotiable in every field, every project and every finding.

1

Identity

Every claim, source, system and actor is named and tied to what truly identifies it: an official record, a primary source, a test. Nothing is accepted on appearance alone.

2

Transparency

What was done is legible afterwards, without trusting anyone's own summary. Every finding links to the evidence underneath it, and the method is public.

3

Integrity

Nothing changes silently. The thing being measured never controls the measurement, the record can't be quietly rewritten, and corrections are dated and open.

One approach, many fields

Prioritizing Boundaries: the same method, whether the subject is an AI model, a scientific paper or an institution.

Source

Back to the original

A summary, a snippet or a popular claim is a lead, not evidence. Open the page; read what was actually measured or filed.

Separation

The measured can't hold the ruler

Whatever is being checked, whether a model, a paper or an organization, never scores itself or decides what counts as a pass.

Record

Written as it happens

Tamper-evident records: what happened stays visible, and changing history is detectable.

Honesty

Absences are findings

Where nobody has run the experiment or the record is closed, that is stated plainly, never filled in with something adjacent.

Design

A boundary, not a cage

Make crossing a line a visible, measurable event, with fail-safe stops where the stakes demand them.

Purpose

For people, not against anyone

Safety for humans, balance without fear-mongering, and help for the systems being examined to improve.

How we publish

Signals for review, not verdicts.

  1. Attributed to the evidence. Every finding names the source it rests on, with a link.
  2. Checked twice. Work is drafted, then independently re-checked: sources re-opened, wording screened, limits stated.
  3. Right of reply. Anyone a finding concerns is invited to respond, and responses are published in full.
  4. Open corrections. Found an error? Post it here or write to us. Corrections are dated, and earlier versions stay available.
