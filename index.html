When a Limit Breaks

Every science has limits. Same 21 things happen when damaged.

We read 1,223 sciences and wrote down what each one's own sources say happens past its limit. Then we set those 21 things beside humanity's mental health crises. Each match is checked against published sources, and the page says where a field describes it that way and where it is only a resemblance.

Predators

Those who prey on others leave the same signs.

We gathered 333 traits and behaviors that published sources record in those who prey on other people, in a home, a workplace or a business. Beside them are 321 behaviors recorded in animals that hunt, trick or live off other animals, so the two can be held side by side and weighed. Beside both are 259 official findings on what systems do to the people inside them, with what the records say stops it. Each one says what it looks like in plain words, with its source. One sign does not make a predator, and the page says what a pattern is.
