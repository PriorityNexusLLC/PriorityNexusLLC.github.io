We read official records and plain rules and show what they say. Every answer links to the record it came from. No opinions, no accusations. If the record is missing, we say so.

Identity · Transparency · Integrity

What we do

The same thing, whatever the subject: a law, a contract, a board, an AI, a scientific claim.

  • Take something people are told to trust. A law. A card agreement. A company's board. An AI acting for someone. A claim in a paper.
  • Put it beside the record. The filing, the statute, the paper, the log. Never a summary of it.
  • Ask the same short questions every time. Can you say no? Can you be heard? Who wrote it? Can an outsider check it? Who answers when it fails?
  • Publish what the record says, with the link. Anyone can open the same record and see the same thing.

Three rules we never break.

1

Identity

A person or organization is named by its official record, never by a look-alike name. No record, no entry.

2

Transparency

Every answer shows the record it rests on. The method is public and the code is open.

3

Integrity

Nothing changes quietly. The thing being measured never measures itself. Corrections are dated and the old version stays.

How

Prioritizing Boundaries: every rule draws a line. We show where the line is and whether a person can cross back.

  • Open the original. A headline or a snippet is a lead, not evidence.
  • Everyone is read alike. A church, a company, an agency, a law and an AI get the same questions and the same standard.
  • Friction is the measure. Pressure with a way out (notice, a hearing, an appeal, a way to sue) is healthy. A door that should open and does not is the finding.
  • A missing record is a finding. It is said plainly, never filled in with something close.
  • Written as it happens. Every change is logged so that rewriting history shows.
  • For people, not against anyone. The records speak. The page never accuses. Anyone named may respond, and the response is posted in full.

How we publish

Signals for review, not verdicts.

  1. Attributed to the evidence. Every finding names the source it rests on, with a link.
  2. Checked twice. Work is drafted, then independently re-checked: sources re-opened, wording screened, limits stated.
  3. Made with AI assistance, and why. Priority Nexus is run by one person, its founder, who is autistic and has ADHD (AuDHD) and is a woman in a man's world. The programs and the AI do the reading, the counting and the drafting at a scale one person could not reach alone. The rules they follow, the records they may use and the decision to publish are hers. It is said here so readers can weigh it, the same way every other interest is said.
  4. Right to respond. No one is asked to comment before a report goes out. Anyone named may respond afterwards, and the response is posted in full, under their name, the day it arrives.
  5. Open corrections. Found an error? Post it here or write to us. Every e-mail is read by a program the moment it arrives and the check begins; the message is posted at once, in full, under the person or company it says it is from, and stays that way unless the check shows otherwise. Contact details are never posted. Corrections are dated, and earlier versions stay available.
