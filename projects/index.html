Rules

Accountability Check

Put a rule through nine questions: can you say no, can you be heard, can you appeal, who wrote it, can an outsider check it, who answers when it fails.

Already read: 79,816 federal and state laws (twenty states), the rulebooks of twenty-three religious and other bodies, card agreements, nonprofit governance answers, withheld contract terms and agency records reports. Rules for children, the elderly, the disabled and immigrants are gathered with a line on who may speak for the person. Paste your own rule and it is read the same way.

Who is tied to whom

PRIGH: the sky

162,895 organizations and people in public life, from filings only: boards, owners, lobbying, PAC money, government posts. Click a name and the records under every link are listed.

Three pictures: the sky, the flows of money and people between ten fields, and the loops where a company lobbied a bill, a member introduced it, and the company's PAC paid that member. A Balance page counts shared boards, single points and long tenures against published standards.

When a Limit Breaks

We read 1,223 sciences and wrote down what each one's own sources say happens when a limit is crossed. The same 21 things kept happening.

The face is plain words. Each science's own terms, its equation and a published source sit in drop-downs. Wikipedia is not used as a source. What we could not find is said.
